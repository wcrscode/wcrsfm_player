<p>"Yesterday's Top Secrets", hosted by Tim Anstaett, is an hour-long show that airs on Thursday and Friday nights at 7:00pm. It features oldies that aren't old, because they haven't been played to death on all the other stations, and alternative music that was simply a pleasant alternative to all of the disco that surrounded it when it first came out.</p>
