<p>Weekly hour-long excerpts from DJ Dynamo's monthly set at Spacebar in Columbus, Ohio. Vortex features the best tunes from the heyday of alternative dance in the 1980s, with special themed parties throughout the year. Virtual Vortex is a distillation of the five hour monthly set into hour-long weekly segments.</p>
