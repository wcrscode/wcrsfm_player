"IAH is the right program at the right time"

A weekly music-based program delivering an inspired reinvention in how we inform ourselves about and experience the world we live in. 

As one listener described, “IAH is as if Democracy Now! was a music show.”

A creatively unconventional and highly engaging form of 'hybrid' programming for our digital age - a soundtrack for our ‘interesting times.’
Each episode is a 57-minute journey of music of various styles and eras, interwoven together in a collage of information and insights, featuring commentary and perspectives that delves into how our world works.

Listeners have called it “entertaining and thoughtful” delivering “great episodes of bracing insight and groovy music!”; noting “Every episode is a great ride that ends too soon.” “It's like a cool mix tape from your friend,” making for a great soundtrack while on the road. "Love the program. The playlists are excellent," and "Love its diversity of genre and perspectives.”
