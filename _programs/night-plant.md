<p><br/><br/>
On air since early 2018, NIGHT PLANT RADIO is WCRS-LP FM's homegrown "background sounds" show. We play ambient, environmental, incidental, production, &amp; spatial music from across decades &amp; traditions: from the 1970s rise of acoustic ecology &amp; environmental soundscape recording to 1980s elevator music to mallwave/mallsoft, weather channel music, &amp; other ambient/environmental imaginaries of the 2010's. NIGHT PLANT mobilizes environmental, spatial, incidental, &amp; background sounds -- often out of or against their context, but with fan &amp; artist communities who dis/locate emplaced sounds in new listening contexts -- to ask big questions about the relationship of listening to place. Yes, we're playing the complete Commander Keen OST (1991-2009) right now. No, we're not very sorry. PS you won't be either.</p>
<p>Sometimes we make our own field recordings &amp; play them. Malls, lakes, you know.</p>
<p>Hosted/produced/badly curated by Jess Lamar Reece Holler, who really is *that* into mallwave.</p>
