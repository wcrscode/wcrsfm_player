The Electric Age with John Horan is a music-driven exploration of technology, history, culture and change. Through storytelling, archival audio and music—primarily from the 1960s and 1970s—each program connects the world we inherited with the one we are creating, exploring not only what is happening, but why it matters and what it may mean for the future.
